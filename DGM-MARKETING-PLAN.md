# DGM Customer Growth Plan
## Brand platform
- Brand: DHE GENIUS MEDIA (DGM)
- Primary tagline: Stay Connected. Stay Genius.
- Campaign headline: Everything Digital. One Genius Platform.
- Core promise: Make everyday digital services easier to discover, order and track.
- Visual system: deep green-black backgrounds, emerald accents, white typography, consistent DGM logo, clear mobile-first layouts.

## Audience priorities
1. Data and airtime buyers who want clear bundle prices and a simple checkout.
2. Existing DGM customers who can be introduced to other currently available services.
3. Small businesses and digital-service resellers who need convenient access to supported services.

## Message pillars
- Convenience: everyday digital services in one place.
- Clarity: show the exact service, price and recipient details before confirmation.
- Confidence: provide clear order status and a visible support contact.
- Connection: use consistent branding and helpful customer communication.

## Four-week launch sequence
### Week 1 — Brand launch
- Publish the refreshed homepage and matching social profile artwork.
- Post a short brand-introduction video: logo/tagline, data and airtime, broader services, customer support, contact details.
- Pin a post explaining what DGM does and how a new customer gets started.
- Use a direct call to action: visit the DGM website or message WhatsApp 0241518385.

### Week 2 — Data and airtime acquisition
- Publish network-specific creatives for MTN, Telecel and AirtelTigo.
- Show actual available bundle sizes and exact prices only after checking the live catalogue.
- Create a simple carousel: choose network → choose bundle → enter recipient → review price → confirm.
- Ask customers to contact support if they need help; do not promise delivery times that have not been verified.

### Week 3 — Introduce other services
- Promote one service category per post (wallet/payments, savings/Susu, entertainment, and other digital services).
- Only advertise features that are enabled and working for customers at the time of publication.
- Explain eligibility, fees and terms clearly where applicable.

### Week 4 — Retention and learning
- Publish a practical tip or service explainer.
- Ask customers for feedback through support channels.
- Review which posts generated website visits, WhatsApp conversations, registrations and completed orders.
- Repeat the strongest-performing creative and improve weak calls to action.

## Suggested posting rhythm
- 3 feed posts per week: brand/service education, focused offer, trust/support explainer.
- 2–4 short status/story updates per week, using the same current offer and contact details.
- 1 short vertical video per week (15–30 seconds), with captions for silent viewing.
- Reply to genuine customer questions promptly and consistently.

## 30-second brand video outline
- 0–3s: DGM logo and “Stay Connected. Stay Genius.”
- 3–8s: Data bundles for MTN, Telecel and AirtelTigo.
- 8–13s: Airtime and other currently available digital services.
- 13–19s: Show the actual mobile website and simple customer journey.
- 19–24s: Show order tracking/support only as it exists in the live product.
- 24–30s: “Everything Digital. One Genius Platform.” WhatsApp 0241518385; calls 0508667776 / 0241518385.

## Conversion and trust checklist
- Check the mobile homepage and every CTA on a real phone.
- Keep registration/sign-in links working.
- Verify displayed services and prices against the live platform before publishing campaigns.
- Make customer support details easy to find.
- Ensure checkout displays the recipient, service, amount and confirmation step clearly.
- Monitor failed orders, refunds and support complaints; resolve operational issues before increasing paid promotion.
- Do not claim guaranteed uptime, instant delivery, cashback, rewards or security certifications unless verified and active.

## Weekly measurement
Track:
- Homepage visits
- Clicks on “Create your DGM account”
- Registrations completed
- Data/airtime checkout starts
- Successful orders
- Repeat customers
- WhatsApp enquiries
- Failed orders and customer complaints

Use a simple weekly table with counts and week-over-week change. Optimize based on completed orders and customer satisfaction, not likes alone.

## Next growth features (only after core ordering is reliable)
1. A transparent referral programme with written eligibility and reward terms.
2. Opt-in promotional messaging with a clear way to stop receiving promotions.
3. Service-specific landing pages and trackable campaign links.
4. Customer feedback prompts after completed orders.
