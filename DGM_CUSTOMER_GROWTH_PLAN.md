# DHE GENIUS MEDIA (DGM) — 30-Day Customer Growth Plan

## Campaign objective

Acquire first-time customers, turn interested visitors into completed orders, and encourage repeat purchases. Start with organic channels and small, measurable tests; do not spend on paid ads until live registration, checkout, payment, and order fulfilment have been tested end to end.

## Before promoting the site: launch gate

- [ ] Confirm the deployed homepage is the current public/index.html version.
- [ ] Test the homepage on an Android phone and a desktop browser.
- [ ] Complete a test registration and sign-in using a test account.
- [ ] Verify Data, Airtime, wallet funding, order history, and support links.
- [ ] Place a low-value test order and confirm provider fulfilment, order status, and wallet debit/refund behaviour.
- [ ] Verify Paystack returns users to the correct page and wallet balances update only after verified payment.
- [ ] Confirm WhatsApp and both telephone links open the correct contact methods.
- [ ] Fix any failed step before buying traffic.

## Weekly execution

| Period | Focus | Actions | Success measures |
|---|---|---|---|
| Days 1–7 | Brand awareness | Publish a launch poster; post a short vertical video; share the website and WhatsApp contact in relevant groups without spamming; ask existing customers to share feedback. | Reach, post engagement, link clicks, WhatsApp enquiries |
| Days 8–14 | Data and airtime | Publish clear network-specific bundle creatives; explain the ordering steps; promote only prices and packages confirmed live on DGM. | Visits to Data/Airtime pages, registrations, completed orders, failed orders |
| Days 15–21 | Cross-sell | Introduce only services that are available and operational; publish short demos and FAQs; use customer questions to guide content. | Service-page visits, enquiries, completed purchases by service |
| Days 22–30 | Retention | Send opt-in follow-ups, request feedback after completed orders, publish answers to common questions, and invite satisfied customers to recommend DGM. | Repeat-customer rate, repeat orders, support resolution time, referrals |

## 30-second promotional video

1. **0–4 seconds:** DGM logo and headline: “Everything Digital. One Genius Platform.”
2. **4–10 seconds:** Show the customer choosing MTN, Telecel, or AirtelTigo data.
3. **10–16 seconds:** Show the customer reviewing an order and available payment options.
4. **16–22 seconds:** Show other services only if currently available and working.
5. **22–27 seconds:** Show a clear mobile view of the DGM website.
6. **27–30 seconds:** End card: “DHE GENIUS MEDIA — Stay Connected. Stay Genius.” WhatsApp 0241518385; calls 0508667776 / 0241518385.

Use captions, high contrast, legible text, and a vertical 9:16 version for status and reels. Do not show real customer details, balances, phone numbers, or payment references.

## Posting rhythm

- 3 short videos or reels each week.
- 3 static posts each week: service explainer, price/package information verified against the live catalogue, and customer FAQ.
- WhatsApp Status most days, with a direct link to the relevant DGM page.
- One weekly customer question/poll to learn what customers need.
- Reply promptly to genuine enquiries and record recurring issues.

## Measurement sheet

Record weekly:
- Content published and reach.
- Website link clicks and unique visits, where analytics are available.
- Registration starts and completed registrations.
- First-time purchasers and completed orders.
- Order failure/refund rate.
- Repeat purchasers and repeat-order rate.
- WhatsApp enquiries and enquiry-to-order conversion.
- Customer complaints and time to resolution.

Use the same reporting period and definitions each week. A visit or registration is not a sale; count a sale only when the order is completed and reconciled.

## Referral programme — future phase

Do not advertise rewards until the rules and tracking are implemented. Before launch, define eligibility, reward amount, payout timing, fraud controls, refund/cancellation handling, and customer-facing terms. Pilot with a small group before broad release.

## Operating rule

Never advertise a feature, price, provider, or delivery promise that has not been verified on the live customer experience. Reliability and transparent support are part of DGM's marketing.
